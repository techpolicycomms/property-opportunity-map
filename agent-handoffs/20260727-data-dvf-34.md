## Known-broken / unverified

- **2019–2020 have no lon/lat** — official geo-dvf `/latest` no longer publishes
  those years (rolling ~5-year window). Raw archive fallback is not geocoded;
  needs BAN/parcelle join (`agent:geo`).
- Raw-archive `mutation_id` is synthetic (DGFiP files lack Etalab `id_mutation`).
- cquest.org is a community mirror of historical DGFiP releases, not a
  data.gouv.fr resource — licence is the same underlying open data, provenance
  is secondary.
- Price-per-m² tails still include extremes up to the 100 000 hygiene cap;
  not audited for multi-lot / dépendance inflation beyond current filters.
- Parquet is local only (gitignored); re-run the CLI on a fresh clone.
